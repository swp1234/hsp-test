An interactive behavioral psychology self-reflection application mapping sensory sensitivity across 5 major sensory channels. Built upon Dr. Elaine N. Aron's Sensory Processing Sensitivity (SPS) framework and the DOES model (Depth of processing, Overstimulation, Emotional reactivity, Sensing subtleties), this tool allows users to explore their sensory thresholds, generate a 1080x1920 Sensory Profile Story Card, and access a 5-minute quiet-zone reset protocol.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **5 Sensory Processing Domains**: Assesses environmental thresholds across Sound/Auditory, Light/Visual, Touch & Texture, Emotional Empathy, and Social Interaction.
- **1080x1920 High-Resolution Story Card**: Generates a deep-violet obsidian canvas card formatted for Instagram Stories, Threads, and mobile camera roll via the Web Share API.
- **5-Minute Quiet-Zone Reset Protocol**: Direct bridge to an interactive sensory recovery tool ([`/hsp-test/reset.html`](https://dopabrain.com/hsp-test/reset.html)) for rapid decompression from sensory overload.
- **Curated Sensory Sensitivity Literature**: References foundational works (*The Highly Sensitive Person* by Dr. Elaine Aron, *Sensitive* by Jenn Granneman & Andre Sólo).
- **100% Client-Side Privacy**: Zero responses, categories, or scores are ever sent to external servers. All evaluation runs locally in the browser runtime.
- **12 Global Languages**: English, Korean, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Russian, Hindi, Indonesian, and Turkish.

## 🔬 Scientific Foundations

Sensory Processing Sensitivity (SPS) is an innate neurobiological trait observed in approximately 15–20% of humans and over 100 other animal species, reflecting a more sensitive central nervous system and deeper cognitive processing of physical, social, and emotional stimuli.

- **Aron, E. N., & Aron, A. (1997)**. *Sensory-processing sensitivity and its relation to introversion and emotionality*. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 73(2), 345–368.
- **Lionetti, F., et al. (2018)**. *Dandelions, tulips and orchids: evidence for the existence of low-sensitive, medium-sensitive and high-sensitive individuals*. Translational Psychiatry, 8(1), 24.
- **Greven, C. U., et al. (2019)**. *Sensory Processing Sensitivity in the context of Environmental Sensitivity: A critical review and agenda for research*. Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews, 98, 287–305.
- **Acevedo, B., et al. (2014)**. *The highly sensitive brain: an fMRI study of sensory processing sensitivity and response to others' emotions*. Brain and Behavior, 4(4), 580–594.
